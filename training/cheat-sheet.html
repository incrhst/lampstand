Sunday, step by step

  1. Screens on. Open Lampstand.
  2. Present: open the service for live.
  3. The idle screen shows until you start.
  4. Space through the service.
  5. Close service at the end.

Phones and tablets

Remotes › Connect a phone or tablet. Run the service: a helper runs it all. Speaker: one item's slides, Next and Back only. Setup › Devices › Revoke.

The five surprises

  2. Order changes: ⇧↓ Enter
  3. Unplanned reading: / then ⇧Enter
  4. Updated deck: choose the new one, Enter
