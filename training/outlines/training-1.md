# Training Session 1: Run a service

For operators. 10 minutes. Ends with: a practice service run with no wrong slide and no desktop showing.

| # | Item | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Header: "Welcome. Press Space to start" | |
| 2 | Video: Welcome (`videos/t1-welcome.json`, about 40 s) | What the trainee will be able to do; the one rule: nothing reaches the screen until you send it. |
| 3 | Deck: The Present screen (`t1-present-screen.pptx`, 4 slides) | Four places to look (the wall, red LIVE, gold NEXT, the sidecard on the right); the one rule; the four keys for today. The trainer points at the real screen. |
| 4 | Video: Going live and moving through slides (`videos/t1-going-live.json`, about 2 min) | Space and arrows, the LIVE and NEXT cards and the notes, Shift+Down and Enter, click a card, the builds panel, verse and chorus keys, Focus (F). |
| 5 | Header: "Practice. Run these as if it were Sunday" | The trainee drives from here. |
| 6 | Song: Amazing Grace (seeded, public domain) | Space through the verses; press 1 to go back to verse 1. |
| 7 | Scripture: John 3:16-18 (KJV) | A reading across slides. |
| 8 | Video: Blank, Idle, Clear (`videos/t1-blank-idle-clear.json`, about 1 min) | Full stop, Idle, Clear text, Show. Blank first, think second. |
| 9 | Deck: Practice: a sermon deck (`t1-sermon-practice.pptx`, 5 slides) | Psalm 23 with click builds (the builds panel shows the next click), Fade transitions and the preacher's notes. Blank once in the middle; try Focus. |
| 10 | Deck: You're ready (`t1-ready.pptx`, 2 slides) | Sunday step by step. Hand over the printed key sheet; Close service. |
