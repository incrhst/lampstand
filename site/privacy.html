Privacy

Lampstand collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your songs, services or settings to us. We have no server that receives them.

The app

Your songs, services, themes and settings are stored on your own computer.

  • Updates. The app checks GitHub for a newer version. GitHub receives your computer's IP address, as it does for any download, and handles it under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Drive and Slides (optional). If you connect a Google account, Lampstand talks to Google directly from your computer, using only the permission to open files you pick (drive.file). The sign-in token is kept in your operating system's keychain. You can disconnect in Lampstand or revoke access in your Google account.
  • Sync relay (optional). If your team sets up a sync relay, your team runs it and controls the data on it. Lampstand does not provide one.
  • Your own network. Lampstand talks to other devices and to OBS on your local network. That traffic does not leave it.

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers. The download buttons ask GitHub for the latest release when the page loads, so GitHub sees your IP address then. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.
