Try it with us

Lampstand is an early version of a free church projection app. We are looking for about ten churches to run it beside their current setup for a few Sundays, with our help, and to tell us plainly what works and what does not.

Who it suits

A church where one volunteer runs the screen and the setup feels like juggling. Most likely you:

  • play sermon slides from PowerPoint or Google Slides,
  • use OpenSong, OpenLP, FreeShow or PowerPoint on its own for songs,
  • and, if you stream, use OBS.

If you rely on MIDI, lighting cues or timecode, Lampstand does not do those yet, and it is better to wait.

What you get

  • A short call to see whether it fits.
  • Help setting it up on your own laptop: your songs and one of your real decks brought in, and your screen fitted so words stay inside its edges.
  • A midweek rehearsal before you use it on a Sunday.
  • A direct line to the person building it. Problems you find are fixed in the open, where you can follow them.

What we ask

  • A volunteer who runs the screen and can spare a few hours in total.
  • Run Lampstand alongside your current tool for a few Sundays. Keep your current tool ready as the fallback until you trust Lampstand.
  • Honest feedback, including what confused you.

What to know first

  • It is free and open source (GPL-3.0), with no paid tier and no account. Your songs and services stay on your own computers.
  • The Windows installer is not signed yet, so Windows asks you to confirm. The install guide shows how.
  • We will not name your church or quote you anywhere without your written permission.
