Decisions

Short summaries of the decision records. Each links to the full write-up, including what was checked and what was rejected.

Records

0001. PPTX reader: borrow from DeckCraft, own the mapping

Proposed · full record

Use DeckCraft’s deckcraft-pptx reader as the XML layer, pinned to a commit, and write our own mapping into lampstand-slides so nothing from it leaks out. Its licence is compatible with GPL-3.0; the risk is its age, so we fall back to our own parser if fidelity on real decks is poor.

0002. Sync repo: wrap Automerge ourselves, not samod

Accepted · full record

samod is wire-compatible with automerge-repo but pre-1.0, async throughout and changing between releases. We build our own repo on automerge directly, with our own storage and sync-state handling.

0003. New service from a list, with a small local model

Proposed · full record

Paste the week’s list of songs and readings and get a draft service to check and create. Parsing runs on this computer with a small Gemma model where available, or a plain line parser otherwise. The model only reads text; Lampstand’s own code finds the songs and passages, and the model never touches what is on screen.

0004. Sync sessions: encrypt on our handshake, not Noise

Accepted · full record

The existing handshake already authenticates both devices but leaves frames unprotected on the church network. We add key agreement and authenticated encryption to that handshake rather than replacing it with Noise.

0005. Live stream: manage OBS, don’t replace it

Accepted · full record

OBS stays the streaming engine. Lampstand supplies graphics as a browser source and controls OBS over its WebSocket, so a volunteer never needs to open OBS during a service.

0006. Tablet remote: a browser page over plain HTTP, paired by QR code and PIN

Accepted · full record

The remote is a page on the local server, opened by QR code on the raw LAN IP and paired with a PIN. Plain HTTP means no secure-context browser features, so the remote is limited to what is safe without them, with HTTPS left as a later addition.

0008. Presenter lease: one device drives the screens, claimed by asking

Accepted · full record

Only presenting is single-holder: one paired device holds a lease (device, name, epoch) passed on the sync connection and merged by newest. Others plan, and must ask the presenter, or wait until it has been offline for the takeover time, before they can take a service live.

All records, including ones added after this page was written, are in docs/decisions.
