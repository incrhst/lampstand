Using Lampstand

Two jobs, Plan and Present, with the one-time configuration under Setup.

Plan, Present and Setup

Plan and Present are the jobs a volunteer does every week. Setup is configured once and checked before a service.

AreaWhat it is for
PlanBuild the service: songs, scripture, slides and media in order. Open for live hands it to Present.
PresentRun the service: next, previous, black, jump to a verse. Also where OBS status, scene buttons and the tablet remote QR code appear.
SetupScreens (the windows Lampstand draws on, each with its own safe area, theme and idle screen), Themes, Church look (name, logo, colours and font), Scripture, Devices and OBS. Team (people and roles) is planned to join them.
HelpQuick tips, the Keys, Help and feedback (the docs, asking a question, reporting a problem, suggesting an idea) and About (the version, and updating it).

Groups and looping

A header in the service order starts a group: the header and the items under it, up to the next header. In Present each group sits in its own outlined box in the service order, with its name above its items.

  • Fold a group. Click the group’s name to fold it up and keep a long order short. Click again to open it. The slide on air always stays visible, even when its group is folded.
  • Loop a group. Press Loop under the group’s name and its slides advance by themselves and start again from the top, which suits the minutes before and after a service. The box brightens and the button reads Looping. Press it again to stop; the group then holds on its last slide.
  • Set the time. While a group is on air, choose how long each still slide stays up (5, 8, 12 or 20 seconds). The change applies from the next slide and belongs to this run of the service. The saved loop is changed in Plan. Videos play through at their own length.
  • See the time left. While a group loops, a thin bar along the bottom of the live screen fills as the slide’s time runs out and starts again on the next slide.

Quick tips

Present shows a short Did you know? card when a feature is in front of you: for example the loop tip when your service has a group, or the keyboard jump tip when a song with several verses is on air. One card shows at a time. Got it dismisses it for good, and a tip for something you have already used never appears. All quick tips opens Help › Quick tips, which lists every tip, including ones you dismissed, each with a short clip.

Songs and imports

Use Get songs for a free song database by language. To bring in your own library, use Import files… or Import folder…. Lampstand detects the format of OpenSong, OpenLyrics, SongSelect, FreeShow and ProPresenter files.

PowerPoint and Google Slides

PowerPoint decks are read directly, so PowerPoint does not need to be installed on the projection laptop. The aim is to keep animation steps, transitions, video and speaker notes, and to show a fidelity report when something can’t be reproduced. Google Slides arrive through a connector.

Themes

A theme decides how songs, scripture and text look: font, size, colour, alignment, background, where the words sit and how many lines go on a slide. Open Setup › Themes to see them. Lampstand starts with one, Starter (white on black), which can’t be deleted but can be reset to how it came.

Pick a theme and change anything: the preview beside it shows a song, a verse and a text slide in the shape of your main screen, and the theme saves as you go. Use New or Duplicate to make your own, Rename to name it, and Delete to remove it (the last theme can’t go). Use as the default sets the theme used wherever none is chosen.

Choose a theme for a whole service with the Theme menu at the top of the service in Plan. Choose one for a single screen in Setup › Screens; a screen’s theme wins over the service’s. A background can be a colour, a picture or a video.

Church brand

The quickest way to a look that is yours is Setup › Church look (also a step in Get started). Type your church’s name, add your logo, pick a main colour, a second colour and a font, and choose Make my theme. Lampstand makes a matching theme and uses it everywhere that doesn’t choose its own. Your logo is what the Logo button in Present puts on the screens, on your main colour.

To share your look, use Export brand on Church look or Themes. It saves one file with your name, logo, colours, font name, themes and idle screen, and the pictures and videos they use. Videos over 150 MB are left out, and Lampstand says so. On another computer or campus, choose Import brand: Lampstand shows what is in the file and asks whether to Add alongside your themes or Replace the ones with the same name. It never overwrites the Starter theme. Fonts are not in the file, only their names, so Lampstand tells you if one isn’t installed on that computer.

Logo bug

A logo bug is your logo drawn small in a lower corner of the screen, the way a broadcaster marks its programmes. Lampstand draws it over songs, scripture, text slides, PowerPoint and Google Slides slides, pictures and video, on every audience screen and in the picture sent to OBS, so the congregation and anyone watching the stream always see it. It uses the logo from Setup › Church look; until you add one the controls are greyed out.

In Setup › Church look, under Logo bug, switch it on, choose the lower left or lower right corner (lower right to begin with), a size (small, medium or large, as a share of the screen’s height) and how solid it is. A sample slide beside the form shows how it will look. It is always kept inside each screen’s safe area, so a projector that crops the edges cannot hide it, and it is scaled to every screen’s own size and shape.

The bug does not move or fade when the slide changes. Blank covers it, and so does the Logo screen, which shows the whole logo instead. It is not drawn on an idle screen that is your logo already, and the stage display never shows it. On the stream it can sit over a lower-third caption in the same corner, so pick the other corner if your captions run wide. During a service, turn it on or off from Present’s More menu without opening Setup; the choice is kept. The bug’s corner, size and opacity are part of the brand file, so they travel with your look to another computer.

Default (idle) screen

When nothing is on air, a screen can show more than black: a picture, a video that loops with the sound off, or one slide of a deck. In Setup › Screens, choose one for all screens under When nothing is on air, and give a single screen its own if it needs a different one. It shows before the service, and again when you end the service.

Logo and Blank are still one key away and cover the idle screen. Present’s live preview shows what the screen is showing, idle screen included.

Live stream with OBS

Lampstand does not replace OBS; it feeds and drives it.

  • Graphics in: lyrics and lower thirds are served as a transparent browser source from Lampstand’s local server.
  • Control out: over the OBS WebSocket (OBS 28 and later), Lampstand shows connection, streaming, recording and dropped-frame status in Present, switches to the scene named on a service item, and offers scene buttons and audio mutes.

Rationale: decision 0005.

Tablet and phone remote

Present shows a QR code for a page on the local server (http://<laptop-ip>:7340/remote by default). Scan it on a device on the same Wi-Fi, enter the PIN, and it controls the service. Nothing is installed on the tablet. Rationale: decision 0006.

Offline and recovery

A whole service runs offline from one laptop. Changes are journaled so the app can recover after a crash. Several computers can share a library through local-first sync; see Sync.

Sync between devices

The bar at the bottom of the window always says where sync with your other computers stands. Click it to open Setup › Devices.

This device onlyNothing is paired, so changes stay on this computer.
Synced with 2 devicesEvery paired device is connected and has your latest changes.
Syncing…Changes are moving to or from a paired device right now.
Offline: 1 device not reachable (amber)A paired device is off, asleep or on another network. Changes wait and sync when it is back.
Sync problem (amber)Sync is not working. Hover over it for the reason, for example that a paired device no longer accepts this one (unpair it on both and pair again), or that the local server could not start.

To pair, open Setup › Devices on the presenting computer and choose Pair a device. On the planning laptop, type the six-digit code in Join another device. If the other computer is not found automatically, also type its address (shown under This device). Devices must be on the same network. The Devices page shows each paired device, why one is not connected, and when a change last arrived and last went out.
