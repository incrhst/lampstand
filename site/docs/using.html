Using Lampstand

Two jobs, Plan and Present, with the one-time configuration under Setup.

Plan, Present and Setup

Plan and Present are the jobs a volunteer does every week. Setup is configured once and checked before a service.

AreaWhat it is for
PlanBuild the service: songs, scripture, slides and media in order. Open for live hands it to Present.
PresentRun the service: next, previous, black, jump to a verse. Also where OBS status, scene buttons and the tablet remote QR code appear.
SetupScreens (the windows Lampstand draws on, each with its own safe area) and Devices. Themes and Team are planned to join them.

Songs and imports

Use Get songs for a free song database by language. To bring in your own library, use Import files… or Import folder…. Lampstand detects the format of OpenSong, OpenLyrics, SongSelect, FreeShow and ProPresenter files.

PowerPoint and Google Slides

PowerPoint decks are read directly, so PowerPoint does not need to be installed on the projection laptop. The aim is to keep animation steps, transitions, video and speaker notes, and to show a fidelity report when something can’t be reproduced. Google Slides arrive through a connector.

Live stream with OBS

Lampstand does not replace OBS; it feeds and drives it.

  • Graphics in: lyrics and lower thirds are served as a transparent browser source from Lampstand’s local server.
  • Control out: over the OBS WebSocket (OBS 28 and later), Lampstand shows connection, streaming, recording and dropped-frame status in Present, switches to the scene named on a service item, and offers scene buttons and audio mutes.

Rationale: decision 0005.

Tablet and phone remote

Present shows a QR code for a page on the local server (http://<laptop-ip>:7340/remote by default). Scan it on a device on the same Wi-Fi, enter the PIN, and it controls the service. Nothing is installed on the tablet. Rationale: decision 0006.

Offline and recovery

A whole service runs offline from one laptop. Changes are journaled so the app can recover after a crash. Several computers can share a library through local-first sync; see Sync.
