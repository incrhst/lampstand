Getting started

From download to your first service in about ten minutes. You do not need a projector to try it.

Download Lampstand

What you need

  • Mac: macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Linux: 64-bit, with a graphics driver that supports Vulkan.
  • A projector or TV is optional. Lampstand can show the congregation’s screen in a window on the laptop, so you can try everything first.
  • Internet is only needed to download and to update. Lampstand runs offline on Sunday morning.

Install

Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Lampstand into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet: choose Open. Lampstand is signed and checked by Apple.

Windows

  1. Run the installer. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”, because the installer is not signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. If it is missing, Windows fetches a small helper (WebView2) the first time, which needs the internet once.

Linux

  • AppImage: make it runnable and start it: 
    chmod +x Lampstand_*.AppImage
./Lampstand_*.AppImage
    If it does not start on Ubuntu 22.04 or later, install libfuse2 first (sudo apt install libfuse2).
  • .deb (Debian, Ubuntu): sudo apt install ./Lampstand_*.deb.
  • Output to a projector needs a working Vulkan graphics driver.

Your first service

When Lampstand opens it shows a Get started strip across the top. Work along it, or follow these steps.

  1. Try the Welcome service. Choose Try the Welcome service, then press Space to go live and Space again to move on. It has two hymns and a verse. ← goes back and . blanks the screen.
  2. Choose your screen. Plug in the projector or TV, open Setup › Screens and choose it under Displays, or add a window to try it on the laptop. Use Calibrate so words never run off the edge of the picture.
  3. Add your songs. In Plan, drop song files onto the library: OpenLP, EasyWorship, ProPresenter, FreeShow, OpenSong, OpenLyrics or a SongSelect export. PowerPoint files and Google Slides links become decks, and pictures and video can be added too.
  4. Plan your first service. In Plan, choose New service. Press / to search: type a song title, or a verse like John 3:16, and press Enter to add it. Drag items to put them in order.
  5. Go live. Choose Open for live. In Present, Space moves on. / quick-adds a song or verse to the running order, even in the middle of the service.

More on each of these is in Using Lampstand.

Updating

Lampstand looks for a new version when it starts and every few hours, and shows Update available at the bottom of the window. Install it from Setup › About. It only installs when nothing is on air and no service starts within the hour, so an update never interrupts one. The Linux .deb does not update itself; download the new one.

Where your data lives

Your songs, services and settings stay on your computer. Back this folder up to keep them safe:

  • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/org.lampstand.app
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\org.lampstand.app
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/org.lampstand.app

Help

  • A question? Ask in Discussions. Other churches and the team answer.
  • Something broken, or an idea? Open an issue.
  • In the app, Setup › About has buttons for all of these that fill in your version for you.
  • Tell us about your church (optional, two minutes) so we know who Lampstand is for and what to build next: the form.

Please leave passwords and OBS Browser source addresses out of anything you post: they contain a key.
