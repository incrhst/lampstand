Lampstand

Order of Worship for every Sunday, on every screen

Lampstand is a free church projection app, built so one laptop can run the whole service: songs, scripture, sermon slides and the live stream. Offline on Sunday morning, fitted to every screen in the room, and simple enough for a volunteer after ten minutes of training.

    Prelude0:45

    Why I built it

    I was working with a team that ran a whole service from a single machine, using OpenSong, OBS, PowerPoint and Zoom. Let’s just say there was a lot of juggling.

    I wanted to recommend a replacement, and I couldn’t find the right thing. So I decided to build it instead.

    Learn more about the author at dbain.com.

    Lampstand reads your PowerPoint and Google Slides, brings your songs across from OpenSong and other apps, and works hand in hand with OBS, so the juggling happens inside one app and one volunteer can run Sunday.

    It is free, it is open source, and it is built in the open with the people who use it. If it is missing something your church needs, tell us.

    Notices

    In this preview

    • Plan a service, then run it from one operator screen, with speaker notes and a next-slide preview
    • Songs and Bibles in, from OpenSong, OpenLP, EasyWorship, ProPresenter, FreeShow and SongSelect
    • PowerPoint and Google Slides decks with their animation steps, and video and images
    • Output windows fitted to every screen, with calibration for the edges
    • OBS: switch scenes with the service, and send lower thirds or the full screen as a Browser source
    • Encrypted sync between your church’s own devices, and updates inside the app

    Still to come

    Free, for good

    GPL-3.0, with no paid tier and no company cloud your Sunday depends on. Your songs and services stay on your own machines, in open formats you can take anywhere.
