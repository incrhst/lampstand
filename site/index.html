Lampstand

Order of Worship for every Sunday, on every screen

Lampstand is a free church projection app, built so one laptop can run the whole service: songs, scripture, sermon slides and the live stream. Offline on Sunday morning, fitted to every screen in the room, and simple enough for a volunteer after ten minutes of training.

In developmentFree and open source (GPL-3.0), planned for Windows, macOS and Linux. Nothing to download yet: we’re building it in the open.

Notices

In the workshop: built so far

  • The data model, and the live engine that moves through a service
  • Layout and a GPU renderer with output windows and per-screen calibration
  • Encrypted sync between the church’s own devices on the local network
  • Song and Bible importers for the common formats
  • A PowerPoint reader, built on the open-source DeckCraft crates
  • The OBS browser source, and the desktop app’s first screens

Still to come

  • The operator view, deck import and the planner
  • Team invites and the self-hosted sync relay
  • Signed installers for Windows, macOS and Linux

Free, for good

GPL-3.0, with no paid tier and no company cloud your Sunday depends on. Your songs and services stay on your own machines, in open formats you can take anywhere.

Lampstand is made in the open at github.com/incrhst/lampstand · GPL-3.0-or-later · Set in Old Standard TT and Courier Prime.
