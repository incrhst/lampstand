    1. Start. Try the Welcome service first. A Get started strip walks you through it.
    2. Plan. Build the order of service from songs, scripture, slides and video. Type John 3:16 and press Enter to add a verse.
    3. Slides. Bring in PowerPoint or Google Slides, with their animation steps and speaker notes.
    4. Present. Shift+Down chooses the next item and Enter starts it. Space moves on. Your notes stay on your screen.
    5. The screen. The congregation sees only the words, inside each screen’s safe area.
    6. OBS. Lyrics go to your live stream as a browser source, with no plugin, and OBS can switch scenes with the service.
    7. Setup. Choose and calibrate each screen once.

    What you get

    Everything Sunday needs, in one place.

    An early preview, built in the open. These work today.

    Plan and run the service

    Build the order of service, then run it from one operator screen with speaker notes and a next-slide preview.

    Why I built it

    Too much juggling.

    I was working with a team that ran a whole service from a single machine, using OpenSong, OBS, PowerPoint and Zoom. I wanted to recommend a replacement, and couldn’t find the right thing. So I built it.

    It is free, GPL-3.0, with no paid tier and no company cloud your Sunday depends on. Your songs and services stay on your own machines, in open formats. Still to come: a signed Windows installer and a Flatpak. If it’s missing something your church needs, tell us. More about the author at dbain.com.
