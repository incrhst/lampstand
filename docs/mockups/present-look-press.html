Look (On air)

  • A bigger picture of the screen, what is live, and what Next shows.
  • While music plays, one line says so.
  • No buttons here, so nothing competes with the picture.

Press (one block)

  • Previous · Next, with Next the biggest thing.
  • Idle · Blank · Clear text as tiles, in the order of their keys , . ;, that show what the screen will become. Blank is no longer in two places.
  • What you added to Ready follows, then "Add to Ready".
  • While sound plays, a Stop the sound tile joins the row.

Narrower right side

  • One labelled button in On air's heading: Narrower / Wider.
  • Narrower makes the whole right side slimmer, so the service gets the room: wider slides, more of them in a row. The picture of the screen shrinks with it.
  • Everything keeps its place and order: Next above Previous, then the tiles two to a row.
  • Kept on that computer, so a church sets it once.

Power users: the modifier chord

  • Left hand holds Ctrl + Option (Ctrl + Alt on Windows and Linux). After a moment, every control shows its key.
  • Right hand stays on its home keys: U I O / J K L are the Ready tiles (I Idle · U Blank · O Clear text), in the same grid as on screen. , . previous / next item. M mute (stop the sound), P play the waiting music, / add, ; the stream.
  • Matched by key position, so the grid keeps its shape on any keyboard.
  • A volunteer who never holds the chord never sees any of it.

Stream (OBS), for a church that streams

  • A labelled Stream button in the press block: what OBS has on air and the next scene.
  • It opens the OBS drawer over the wall: scenes as big buttons, what the next item switches to, microphone and music into the stream. Close or Esc.
  • A church without OBS never sees it.

Everything else, quietly

  • Add a song or reading: at the end of the service, or press /.
  • Music: Play on the item; it becomes Stop.
  • Check: "All set" in the status bar; amber with the fix when something is wrong.
