Present's right column

One rule for a volunteer who comes once a month: nothing moves and nothing disappears. The same three parts, in the same order, every time.

TOPOn air

  • The screen picture, what is live, and Previous · Next · Blank.
  • Can't be hidden. It's the one thing an operator must always see.

MIDDLELibrary & controls

  • Find a song, Screen, Sound, Remotes, Clear, Keys. Scrolls between the other two.
  • F (or Hide) hides just this part for more room. On air and Ready stay where they are.

BOTTOMReady

  • Blank, Idle, and anything added with ☆ Add to Ready, one tap away.
  • Fold turns it into a single line, "☆ Ready · 3", in the same place. It never slides off the screen. Click the line to open it.

Every service starts the same

When a service is opened for live, everything is open. Last month's folding or hiding never carries over.

What training can say

  1. "What's on the screen is top right."
  2. "Blank, Idle and your extras are bottom right."
  3. "Everything else is in between."
